# About & Feedback

GoWherer is an open-source journey timeline app: depart, record, and revisit — turning every trip into a shareable timeline. It is developed by [@dsjerry](https://github.com/dsjerry) with Expo / React Native and released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/dsjerry/gowherer/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Why This Project Exists

Trips and commutes produce lots of fragments — photos, locations, fares — scattered across albums and chat histories, hard to piece back into one complete journey. GoWherer collects them into a single timeline ordered by time: capture as you go, let the app compute distance and costs when you arrive, and export a PDF or long image whenever you want to share.

All data stays on your own device — no account, no cloud, no ads.
