features:
  - title: Quick Recording
    details: Start a journey and record text, location, photo, video, and audio with one tap so every moment is captured.
  - title: Template Recording
    details: Built-in templates for "Departure", "Arrival", "Rest", and "Checkpoint" — one tap to insert preset text and tags, with separate collections for Travel and Commute modes.
  - title: Transportation Cost
    details: Tag each entry with a transport mode and fare, auto-sum the journey total, and export a detailed cost table.
  - title: Review & Detail
    details: Browse finished journeys as a summary list, then open the detail page to revisit the timeline, media, and track map.
  - title: Stats & Segments
    details: Automatically calculate total distance, duration, average speed, and location points — or pick start/end entries to see per-segment distance, duration, and speed.
  - title: Route Visualization
    details: Continuously record GPS tracks in the background with smoothing; preview routes on a compact map that expands in place, with segment highlighting.
  - title: Export & Share
    details: Export a PDF or a long image with one tap, choosing between "Classic" and "Compact" report templates with track map, stats, and cost breakdown.
  - title: Smart Positioning
    details: Automatically get location info and convert coordinates to readable place names via Amap reverse geocoding, accelerated by a local grid cache and request deduplication.
  - title: Data Safety
    details: One-tap backup and restore; media files are cascade-cleaned on delete, with orphan scanning and storage cleanup in Settings.
